Privacy Policy for ScreenRecorder

ScreenRecorder is a personal tool. It records the user's own screen and
uploads the recordings to the user's own Google Drive using the
drive.file scope, which only gives it access to files it creates.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. OAuth
tokens are stored only on the user's own computer, in a local config
file. No data is sent to the developer or any third party other than
Google Drive.
